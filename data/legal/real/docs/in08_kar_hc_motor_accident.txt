JUDGMENT
This appeal under Section 173(1) of the M.V. Act arises from the judgment and award dated 22.9.2018, passed by the learned Senior Civil Judge, Iternate Court, Jagalur.
2. The appellant/claimant, who sustained injuries in a road traffic accident on 24.08.2013 filed a claim petition under Section 166 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1989, seeking just and proper compensation. The Tribunal awarded a total compensation of Rs.4,41,299/- with interest at the rate of 8% p.a. Being dissatisfied with the quantum of compensation, the claimant is before this Court.
4. The accident occurred in the year 2013, and the claimant was aged 35 years at the time of the accident. In the absence of proof of income, the notional income is assessed at Rs.8,000/- per month, according to the chart prepared by the Karnataka Legal Services Authority, as opposed to Rs.7,000/- assessed by the Tribunal.
5. The doctor, who was examined as P.W.2 assessed the permanent physical disability to a particular limb at 45%. The Tribunal has rightly assessed the functional disability at 15%.
6. The claimant admittedly sustained swelling and deformity of left thigh, CLW of about 5 X 5 cm over medial side of lower part of left thigh, CLW over left foot, Supracondylar fracture of left femur. The claimant was inpatient for 22 days and also underwent surgery.
8. In all, the appellant/claimant is entitled for the total compensation of Rs.5,03,098/- as opposed to Rs.4,41,299/- awarded by the Tribunal. The enhanced compensation is Rs.61,799/-.
