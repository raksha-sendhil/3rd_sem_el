Background
5. The Claimant began working for the Respondent in 1997. By 2019 she was employed as an Executive Officer and worked as a Work Coach at Redbridge Jobcentre Plus. She was dismissed with effect from 11 August 2020.
7. On 2 December 2019 the Claimant's adult son applied for Universal Credit. The Claimant was not his Universal Credit adviser. However, on 3, 4, 10, 12 and 17 December 2019 she accessed his Universal Credit account on the Respondent's systems. The Claimant did not dispute the fact that she had no legitimate business reason or any authorisation to do so.
8. On 20 December 2019 the Claimant told her line manager, Ms Tanner, that she had accessed her son's Universal Credit account. On 2 January 2020 the matter was referred internally to Counter Fraud and Investigation. An investigation report was completed on 29 May 2020. Disciplinary proceedings followed.
9. Mrs Reilly was appointed as the Respondent's disciplinary decision-maker. A disciplinary hearing took place on 23 June 2020.
10. By letter dated 10 August 2020 Mrs Reilly summarily dismissed the Claimant for gross misconduct. The Claimant appealed. Following an appeal hearing before Mrs Billings on 25 September 2020 the appeal was dismissed.
11. The Claimant presented her claim to the Tribunal on 9 January 2021. The final hearing took place between 10 and 12 January 2024.
13. The Tribunal set out the following facts regarding the Claimant's personal and health issues at the time that she committed the conduct which led to her dismissal:
"The Claimant experiences multiple disabilities which impact on her health and daily life. Her disabilities include urticaria, asthma, depression, and work-related stress. By the time of her dismissal the Claimant had experienced depression for about 10 years. She was prescribed paroxetine to treat her depression. Around June or July 2019 the Claimant's prescription was set at 10mg. In November 2019 her prescription was increased to 20mg. Paroxetine had side effects for the Claimant of drowsiness and problems sleeping, and the increase in dosage caused her cognitive difficulties. The Claimant's son has some degree of learning difficulties. The Claimant assists him with various aspects of his life."
