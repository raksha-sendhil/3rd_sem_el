RAVI, J. -------------------------------------------- AR.No.228 of 2023 -------------------------------------------Dated this the 16th day of January, 2024 ORDER The arbitration request is made on the basis of Annexure-A1 agreement between the petitioner and the respondent. It relates to a tenancy. According to the respondent, the period of agreement was long over and the request is barred by limitation. It is also stated that the notice itself was sent only recently. The counsel for the petitioner submitted that the respondent had vacated the premises only in May, 2023 and as such, the question of limitation does not arise. It is also submitted that similar cases have already been referred for arbitration. The order in A.R.No.51 of 2023 is placed before me for reference. It is further submitted that the reference made as per the order in A.R.No.51 of 2023 has already begun and another reference which is pending before Sri.P.Mohandas as Arbitrator is yet to begin.
