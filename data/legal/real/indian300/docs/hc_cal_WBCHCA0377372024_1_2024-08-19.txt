Order dictated by Arijit Banerjee, J.:

1.

The petitioner renews his prayer for bail which was rejected earlier on January 4, 2024.

2.

Allegedly 49 bottles of cough syrup containing codeine phosphate were recovered from a scooty belonging to the petitioner.

The petitioner says that there is nothing on record to show that he is the owner of the scooty. He is in custody for one year and three months. Witness action has not yet begun. Charge has only been framed. There is no possibility of early conclusion of the trial. He prays for bail on the ground of delay in progress of the trial.

3.

While opposing the prayer for bail, learned Advocate for the State draws our attention to the materials in the case diary.

4.

It cannot be said that there is no incriminating material at all against the petitioner. However, there is nothing on record to connect the scooty in question with the petitioner. It appears that no efforts have been made by the prosecution to obtain any document of ownership of the scooty.
