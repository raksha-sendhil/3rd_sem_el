Nilesh Baburao Gitte v.
State of Maharashtra (Criminal Appeal No. 1471 of 2013) 07 October 2025 [K.V. Viswanathan* and K. Vinod Chandran, JJ.] Issue for Consideration Appellant was convicted for matricide-killing of one’s own mother. By the present appeal, the appellant calls in question the correctness of the judgment of the High Court.
Headnotes†
