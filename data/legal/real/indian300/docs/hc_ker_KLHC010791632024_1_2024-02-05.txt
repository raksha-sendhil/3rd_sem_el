JUDGMENT
The petitioner herein is the management in ID No.15 of 2012 on the files of the Labour Court, Kannur. The aforementioned proceedings were initiated based on a reference made by the appropriate Government to determine the question as to whether the workman has been denied employment by the petitioner.
2. The petitioner states that he was not able to appear before the Labour Court, and hence, the case was decided ex parte. He has preferred separate applications for setting aside the ex parte award, for condonation of delay and for keeping in abeyance the execution proceedings. The grievance of the petitioner is that no decision has yet been taken. It is on these assertions that this writ petition is filed seeking directions.
3. I have heard Sri. Abdul Raoof pallipath, the learned counsel for the petitioner.
4. In the nature of the order that I propose to pass, notice to the party respondent is dispensed with.
