ORAL ORDER

1.

Draft amendment is allowed. To be carried out forthwith.

2.

The present application is filed under Section 24 of the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908 by the applicant, to transfer Family Suit No.11 of 2019 pending with the learned Family Court, Morbi to any other appropriate Court.

3.

Heard learned advocate for the applicant. Rule.

4.

It is noted that the matter was initially transferred from the Family Court, Ahmedabad to the Family Court, Morbi as the wife is residing at Morbi. Before the C/MCA/1159/2023 ORDER DATED: 06/02/2024 learned Family Court, Morbi, the pleadings are completed by the parties and the matter is kept for pronouncement of judgment. At this stage, the learned Family Court, Morbi has released the matter on the ethical ground that one of the party is known to his father. There is only one Court available at Family Court, Morbi having the jurisdiction to try such matters. Under the peculiar circumstances, it would be appropriate to transfer the proceedings in question before the learned Family Court, Rajkot.
