Disputes have arisen between the parties out of agreement dated 23rd July, 2019. Clause No.20 of the agreement dated 23rd July, 2019 provides for resolution of the disputes by arbitration. Learned counsel for the parties agree that the disputes between the parties can be referred to a sole Arbitrator.

2]

Both parties agree that, Justice S.P.Deshmukh, former Judge of this Court can be appointed as an Arbitrator to decide the disputes arising between them.

3]

Accordingly, the arbitration application is disposed of with following order :

a] Appointment of Arbitrator :-

17.22AA

Mr.S.P.Deshmukh, Retired Judge, is appointed to act as sole arbitrator to adjudicate upon the disputes between the parties.
