JUSTICE GITA GOPI Date : 23/04/2024 ORAL ORDER
1. Heard learned advocates for the parties.
2. The appeal is filed challenging the judgment and award dated 31.3.2009 passed by the learned Motor Accident Claims Tribunal in MACP no.1098/97 for the compensation granted of Rs.2,24,500/-.
However, the appellant has restricted the claim in appeal to Rs.1,12,250/-.
3. Considering the smallness of amount, this Court finds no reason to interfere in the impugned judgment and award passed by the Tribunal. The C/FA/2925/2009 ORDER DATED: 23/04/2024 appeal, accordingly, is disposed of. Notice/Notice of admission is discharged. Interim relief, if any, shall stand vacated.
4. It is made clear that this order would have no bearing and/or shall not be considered as precedent in any of the matters connected to the accident in question vis-a-vis the impugned judgment and award.
5. Since the main appeal is disposed of, connected applications, if any, would not survive and are disposed of accordingly.

Maulik (GITA GOPI,J)
