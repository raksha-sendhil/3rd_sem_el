Dictated by Arijit Banerjee, J.

The petitioners renew their prayer for bail which was rejected earlier on more than one occasion. The last rejection was on January 25, 2024.

Since the petitioners suffered rejections on several occasions, on merits, and we find no change of circumstance, we are not inclined to allow the petitioners’ prayer for bail.

Even otherwise, given the nature of charge and the prima facie involvement of the petitioners as would appear from the statements of eyewitnesses recorded under Section 164 of the Code of Criminal Procedure, we are not inclined to allow the petitioners’ prayer for bail.

Signed By : DEBAJYOTI DAS High Court of Calcutta 4 th of September 2024 11:38:43 AM However, the petitioners are in custody for about a year. PW 1 has already been examined. In view of the fact that the petitioners have been in judicial custody for quite some time, we direct the learned trial Court to expedite the trial to the fullest extent possible and conclude the same at an early date and definitely within a year from the next date fixed for recording of evidence.
