JUSTICE SUSMITA PHUKAN KHAUND Date : 24-05-2024 ORDER Heard Ms. M. Das, learned Legal Aid Counsel for the appellant/petitioner, who has filed an application for leave to file an appeal against acquittal vide judgment dated 07.12.2023 passed by the learned Additional Sessions Judge (FTC), Kokrajhar Circuit Court at Gossaingaon in Session Case No. T1/21/2021 acquitting the respondent Nos. 2 and 3 from the charges under Sections 304(B)/498(A) of the Indian Penal Code, 1860.

Leave to file an appeal against acquittal as per proviso of Section 372 of the CrPC is allowed. The appellant is a victim as per Section 2(wa) of the CrPC. It is submitted on behalf of the appellant that despite overwhelming evidence, the trial Court acquitted the accused. The trial Court has thus erred while passing the impugned judgment.

The delay has already been condoned.

Registry to register the connected appeal in its usual course.

In the above terms, this Criminal Leave Petition stands disposed of.
