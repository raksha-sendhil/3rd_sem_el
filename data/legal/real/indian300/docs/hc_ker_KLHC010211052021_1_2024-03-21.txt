This Claim coming on for hearing on this day in the presence of Smt.S.Jasmine, Standing Counsel for the Official Liquidator and the Respondent being absent and set ex-parte, this Court delivered the following:-

Company Claim No.51 of 2021 in CP.No.13 of 2014
T.R.RAVI.J ------------------------------------------------------- Company Claim No.51 of 2021 in CP.No.13 of 2014 -------------------------------------------------------- Dated this the 21st day of March, 2024 JUDGMENT The Liquidator has filed claim affidavit dated
25.09.2020, seeking a decree against the respondent for a sum of Rs.10,000/-, along with interest at the rate of 12% per annum on the principal sum from 10.05.2012 till the date of decree; and thereafter at the rate of 12% on the said sum, till the date of realisation.
2. There is no appearance for the respondent in spite of valid service of summons, nor is there any opposition to the claim. The respondent is hence declared ex parte.

Company Claim No.51 of 2021 in CP.No.13 of 2014
3. Since there is no contest to the claim made, this application is allowed and an amount of Rs.10,000/- is decreed against the respondent, to be paid along with interest at the rate of 4% per annum on the said sum from 10.05.2012 till realisation.
