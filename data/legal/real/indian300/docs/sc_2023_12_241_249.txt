M/S. BAJAJ ALLIANCE GENERAL INSURANCE CO LTD. v.
RAMBHA DEVI AND ORS. (Civil Appeal No. 841 of 2018)
SEPTEMBER 13, 2023
[DR. DHANANJAYA Y CHANDRACHUD, CJI, HRISHIKESH ROY, PAMIDIGHANTAM SRI NARASIMHA, PANKAJ MITHAL AND MANOJ MISRA, JJ.] HEADNOTES Issue for consideration: Whether a person holding a driving licence in respect of a “light motor vehicle” could on the strength of the licence be entitled to drive a “transport vehicle of light motor vehicle class” having unladen weight not exceeding 7500 kgs.
Motor Vehicles Act 1988 - Person holding a driving licence in respect of a “light motor vehicle” – Entitlement to drive a “transport vehicle of light motor vehicle class” having unladen weight not exceeding 7500 kgs, on the strength of that licence:
