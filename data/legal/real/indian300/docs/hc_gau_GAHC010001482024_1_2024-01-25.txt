Heard Ms. U. Nanda, learned counsel appearing for the accused petitioner as well as Mr. D. Das, learned Addl. P.P., Assam appearing for the State respondent.
By this petition under Section 439 Cr.P.C., the accused-petitioner, namely, Md. Mustafa Ali @ Sheikh, has prayed for grant of bail in connection with Dhekiajuli P.S. Case No.233/2023 under Sections 302/34 of the IPC, corresponding to G.R. Case No. 1861/2023.
The case diary, as called for, is placed before the Court.
The accused petitioner has been in judicial custody since 24.11.2023.
The case of the prosecution is that about one year ago, the informant’s mother with the help of accused No. 2, namely, Md. Hazrat Ali, ran away and got married with the present petitioner. It is alleged that the informant’s mother had expired and it is suspected that the accused person intentionally killed her. Accordingly, a case was registered.
Ms. Nanda, learned counsel for the petitioner, submits that there was love affair between the petitioner and the informant’s mother, who, out of affection, ran away and after she repented of being ran away with the petitioner committed suicide by consuming poison. She further submits that the petitioner is not in any way connected with the incident and he is innocent.
