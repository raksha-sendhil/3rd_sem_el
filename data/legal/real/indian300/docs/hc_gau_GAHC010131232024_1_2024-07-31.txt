2. This application under Section 438 of the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973 has been filed by the petitioners, namely, 1. Mr. Mukul Chandra Ray and 2. Nibash Roy, who are apprehending their arrest in connection with Golakganj P.S. Case No. 58/2024 under Section 120(B)/379/186/353/506 of the Indian Penal Code, 1860.

3. The gist of accusation in this case is that one Munna Sharma, Forester I of Chagolia Block Forest, Account Office had lodged an FIR on 20.03.2024, before the Officer-In-Charge of Chagolia Police Outpost, inter alia, alleging that on receipt of an information that some illegal transportation of sand and chip is taking place in Khathaltoli Ghat since long without valid documents. The informant along with the Forest staff went to Kaymari Part II area and found one Tata 407 truck and a tractor while sand being loaded in the said vehicles.
