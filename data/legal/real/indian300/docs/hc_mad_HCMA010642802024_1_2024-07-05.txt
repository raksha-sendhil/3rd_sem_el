2. The petitioner is facing trial in C.C.No.08 of 2016 on the file of the Additional Chief Metropolitan Magistrate, Egmore for the offences under Section 509 of IPC and Section 4 of TNPHW Act, 1998.
3.The petitioner who is a party-in-person appeared before this Court and submitted that out of 60 witnesses, only 31 witnesses have been examined. Since this Court, by an order dated 19.09.2022 in Crl.O.P.No.21931 of 2022, directed the trial Court to complete the trial within a period of six months from the next hearing date ie., on 11.10.2022, the trial has not been completed till date. Hence, the present petition has been filed seeking a direction to the learned Chief Judicial Magistrate to expedite the trial proceedings.
