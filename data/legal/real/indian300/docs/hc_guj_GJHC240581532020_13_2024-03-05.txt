ORAL ORDER
Learned advocate Mr.Vaghela submits that during the pendency of this appeal from order, the trial is commenced.
Considering the fact that the matter is pending since 2020 at admission stage and the appeal is essentially against order passed below Exh.5 and that now the trial is commenced, it is expected that the trial shall be proceeded as expeditiously as possible. Therefore, this appeal from order is otherwise now not required to be entertained.
Accordingly, this appeal from order is disposed of in view of the subsequent development and it is open for the C/AO/135/2020 ORDER DATED: 05/03/2024 parties to request the trial court to expedite the trial, if it is not completed within reasonable period. It is clarified that this Court has not expressed any opinion on merits and all the contentions of the parties are kept open.
As the appeal from order is disposed of, civil application does not survive and is accordingly disposed of.
