ORAL ORDER
1. The petitioner is before this Court seeking for the following reliefs:
a) “Direct the respondent company be wound up as it is unable to pay it’s debt.
b) Appoint the official liquidator to take charge of affairs of the respondent company.
c) Pass such other orders as this Hon’ble court deems fit.”
2. During pendency of the above matter, a cheque has been issued by respondent-Company. The same has been honoured and the amounts due to the petitioner has been fully and finally settled. Hence, nothing survives for consideration in the above Company Petition and the same stands dismissed as settled.
