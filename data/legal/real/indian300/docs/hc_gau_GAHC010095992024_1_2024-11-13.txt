Baruah, learned Addl. Public Prosecutor, Assam for the State respondent. This application under Section 482 of the Code of Criminal Procedure read with Section 438 of the Code has been filed by the petitioner in respect of the order dated 06.04.2024, passed by the learned Chief Judicial Magistrate, Tinsukia in PRC Case No. 1054/ 2023.

The petitioner was declared proclaimed offender in this case under Sections 419/ 420/ 468 of the IPC showing him as an absconder. The petitioner claims that he was working at Hyderabad and he could not come to the court and in the meantime, the trial court issued P/A against him and ultimately filed case against the petitioner.
Now the petitioner submits that he is interested in facing the trial but apprehends that whenever he will appear before the trial Court he will be remanded back to the custody.
I have heard Mr. R.J. Baruah, learned Addl. Public Prosecutor.
I have considered the submissions of both the parties.
Since the petitioner is interested in facing the trial, this Court is of the opinion that for the ends of justice he deserves to be given an opportunity to face trial.
