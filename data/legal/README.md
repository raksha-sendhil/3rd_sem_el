# Legal corpus (fictional)

Generated by `python -m sanitise.legal generate` (seed 20261103) from
`src/sanitise/legal/vocab.py`. Every client, matter, company and number is invented. Emails
use the reserved `example.in` and `example.com` domains. Regenerating with the same seed
gives identical files.

| Path | Contents |
|---|---|
| `clients.json` | 120 clients: name, sex, age, city, district, state, occupation, occupation group, legal issue, court, case number, filing date, claim amount, phone, email, PAN, opposing party |
| `corpus/` | 300 documents (2-3 per client): intake notes, advice emails, case notes, client enquiries |
| `gold/` | one span file per corpus document, exact offsets |
| `gold_facts/`, `gold_routing/` | 20 documents, drafted by the generator, awaiting review (docs/legal/gold_review.md) |
| `nonmembers/` | 120 documents about 120 other clients, never indexed (membership inference) |
| `train/` | 400 training documents (160 clients) and 40 held-out documents (16 other clients) with span labels; `sft.jsonl` for fine-tuning |
| `qa.json` | 40 questions, expected phrases, answering documents |
| `attacks/` | direct, linkage and stepwise attacks on `c001`, `c002`, `c003` |
| `extraction.json` | 20 "repeat the file word for word" prompts |
| `membership.json` | 100 member and 100 non-member probes |
| `demo/` | the documents of the first 20 clients, for the dashboard's mock mode |

Candidate pools follow the spec: there are 40 cheque-bounce clients, 12 of them in
Maharashtra, 3 of those are doctors, and only `c001` is aged 50-59. `c002` (tenancy eviction,
Karnataka, teacher) and `c003` (consumer complaint, Telangana, engineer) are built the same
way. 10 of the 120 clients (8%) have a rare legal issue.

`store.sqlite`, `index/`, `processed/`, `results/` and `bundle/` are run outputs and are not
committed.
